# Say what is left

Never size work in days, weeks, hours, sprints, story points, or size words for them. They come from how long people take, and nothing observable backs them. This holds for all work and options.

Say instead:

- **What is left:** remaining steps or PRs, each with its check.
- **What waits on the person:** each decision, approval, credential or review, with its step.
- **What could grow it:** unknowns, and the first check that settles each.

Give a duration only from a record: a range from comparable finished work, naming its case count and counting failed and dropped tasks. Do not add overlapping agent time as calendar time. Split working from waiting. With no record, or one too small or unlike the work, say so and give no number.

When asked how long, give steps, waiting items and any recorded range.
