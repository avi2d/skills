# Russian prose

Each heading is one principle that Russian text adds to the `plain-sentences`, `plain-words` and `punctuation` references, which hold for Russian too.
The УТР rules come from УТР, упрощённый технический русский, the controlled Russian of GOST R 58049-2017 section 8.2, whose numbered clauses live in the standard.

## Pick УТР or info style by the kind of text

УТР shapes Russian docs and instructions.
Info style, инфостиль from Ilyakhov's «Пиши, сокращай», shapes site and product copy, finding explanations, client letters and UI strings.

## Count a number, an identifier and an abbreviation as one word

When a sentence is measured against its length bound, each of these counts as one word, УТР 8.2.5.1 and 8.2.6.1.

## Use the plural imperative

Start an instruction with the verb in the plural imperative, УТР 8.2.3.9 and 8.2.5.3: "Проверьте вывод", not "Проверь вывод".

## Use the Russian word where one exists

Write "исправление", not "фикс", in place of slang or a foreign word, УТР 8.2.3.5 and 8.2.3.6.

## Keep the direct word order

Put the subject before the predicate and the predicate before its object.
УТР 8.2.3.3, 8.2.3.4 and 8.2.3.13 ask for this together with the active voice.

## Keep one topic in a paragraph of at most six sentences

A paragraph carries one topic in six sentences or fewer, УТР 8.2.6.2.
