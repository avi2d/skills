# The visitor's job

Name what the visitor's success looks like on this surface before choosing how expressive the surface is.
The job belongs to the surface rather than the product.
A tool's landing page persuades, a fashion house's documentation is read, and a docs index is read too.
Write the job into the surface's design brief and leave it there, because two surfaces of one product can hold different jobs.

## The four jobs

| Job | The visitor | Surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persuade | decides and acts, and the design is what they judge | landing pages, marketing, campaigns, pricing, a personal home page |
| Operate | completes a task | app screens, dashboards, editors, admin, settings, forms |
| Read | understands something | docs, articles, guides, help, changelogs, a report |
| Experience | is inside the work itself | portfolios, galleries, showcases |

## Persuade

- The opening makes the offer intelligible and desirable, exposes a clear action, and shows something only this subject can prove.
- The action is the one the category's visitors came to take, in its working form, such as a contact route that works from the first screen rather than a decorative link toward it.
- The visual world may carry the page, and the page still works as a page: navigation reads as links, copy reads as set text, and the primary action is plainly clickable.
- Display type may carry the voice, and motion may carry one rehearsed focal sequence.

## Operate

- Someone who uses tools of this kind knows where everything is before they notice the style.
- The style lends type, palette, density and one signature move, and never the layout, the navigation model or the controls.
- Controls are the standard ones the visitor knows, web components on the web and native controls in an app.
- Scanability, consistency and the real usage scene outrank expression, and type keeps a stable role scale.
- Motion serves feedback, state and continuity, fast and without load choreography.

## Read

- The reader's question leads, and wayfinding stays intact at every step, so a reader always knows where they are and where to go next.
- The style owns the frame, such as the masthead, the rail and the section openers, and the reading column stays calm.
- The column holds a reading face at real contrast, a measure of about 60 to 75 characters and body text near 16 px.
- Code, tables, notes and figures sit in the standard forms readers know.

## Experience

- The work leads from the first viewport, and the interface recedes around it.
- Navigation stays findable and the work stays reachable by keyboard and touch, however quiet the chrome is.

## Judging a surface against its job

Ask the questions of the surface's own job.
Persuade: with the name covered, does the opening still say who this is for, what is offered and how to act?
Operate: could the visitor do the task from this screen, and is it at least as clean as the category standard?
Read: can a reader find and understand the answer, and read the body comfortably?
Experience: does the work lead, and does the interface stay out of its way?

No program can decide the right job or how expressive a surface should be.
A check can only require that a surface's brief declares one.
